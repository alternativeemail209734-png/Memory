              📚 How To Play (Live Game)
              1. ✍️Type two card numbers in chat, e.g. 1 5
              2. 🃏Both cards flip over. If the pictures match, they stay open
              3. ❌If they don't match, they flip back after a moment
              4. 💡A comma works too: 1, 5
              5. ⭐Each pair you find earns 1 point. The leaderboards keep count
              6. 🔥Match pairs back-to-back for a combo streak - each one in a row is worth more, up to +4 total. A wrong guess resets your streak
              7. ⏰No time limit - the clock just shows how long each game takes
              🃏 New To Memory? Rules Of The Game
              1. 🎴Every card starts face down and shows only its number
              2. 👀Each picture is hidden on exactly two cards. Those two make a pair
              3. 🧠When two cards flip and don't match, they turn back. Remember where they were
              4. 🏆The game is won when every pair has been found
